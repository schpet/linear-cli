## Format addendum

`graphql.groups` may contain an ordered `steps` list or concurrent `lanes`, each with an ordered `steps` list and a bounded first-step overlap deadline. Steps are discriminated by `kind: "graphql" | "asset"`. An asset declares GET or PUT, exact path and query, required and forbidden headers, request body bytes, and response status/headers/bytes. Redirects use only `response.location`: it must be an origin-relative safe path, occurs exactly with a 3xx GET, and targets the next step in that lane or ordered group. `expectedRequests` counts GraphQL and asset steps together. The server logs the matched kind separately and fails closed on an unexpected or out-of-order request.
