## Contract

Candidate descriptors default to `frozen-deno`. The explicit `rust-3.0.0-alpha.1` contract requires an executable. A case's deviation binding supplies an ID, that contract and the SHA-256 of its derived `rust-goldens/<contract>/<caseId>.json` path. The loader checks every case and golden before filtering and before namespace preflight: path/file safety, hash, strict format version 1 and identity, exact changed-surface declarations, orphan files and complete GraphQL User-Agent coverage. A golden can override only the complete Rust argv, complete current expected object, and exact GraphQL User-Agent. It cannot override env, stdin, cwd, timeout, cap or fixture effects.

The runner executes the original Deno case first. A passing baseline allows a fresh Rust candidate view; the equality comparator is unchanged. Four statuses remain (`pass`, `fail`, `not-implemented`, `baseline-drift`), with `reviewedDeviationPasses` and its `reviewedGraphqlUserAgentPasses` subset reported within `pass`. The latter counts only passing cases whose sole approved surface is `graphql-user-agent`; a case that also changes argv is excluded. The report records contract, deviation ID, golden SHA, approved surfaces and separate baseline/candidate observation and fixture facts without raw output bytes. `--propose` retains its baseline projection behavior and now rejects an output path inside the derived golden tree, including a symlinked or not-yet-created descendant.

The generated baseline `GraphQLFixtureSchema` remains frozen at `schpet-linear-cli/2.6.0`. After baseline resolution, the candidate gets a complete cloned runtime fixture with only GraphQL identity `userAgent` changed to `schpet-linear-cli/3.0.0-alpha.1`; assets, requests, effects and responses stay exact. Runtime type signatures in `graphql-server.ts`, `lane-scheduler.ts` and `compare.ts` widen the User-Agent field only for this resolved view. This is the narrow file-list expansion from the microplan. No legacy `fixtureServer` User-Agent claim is made.

The 11 committed GraphQL cases contain five asset steps: two in `document-fixed-host-both`, two in `document-fixed-host-redirect`, and one in `issue-attach-signed-upload`. None pins `User-Agent` in required or forbidden asset headers. R01H rewrites no asset header expectation; a later asset-specific version change would need separate evidence and review. The frozen-only F02B fixed-host driver and transport case corpus are unchanged.

`R01H-GRAPHQL-UA` repeats across 11 case pairs, each with its own SHA-pinned golden. The old/new example, reason and migration guidance are in `rust/compatibility.md`. The plan received independent Claude **SHIP** feedback after revision. Whole-diff review returned **REVISE** for missing negative coverage and a proposal-path edge; those items were addressed, and targeted Claude closeout returned **SHIP**.
